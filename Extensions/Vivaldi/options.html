Switchboard bookmark sync

Keep Switchboard and Vivaldi bookmarks in sync on this Mac. Titles, web addresses, and folders sync; notes, tags, and favorites stay in Switchboard. Both apps must be running.

Bookmark scope

Or select any combination of bookmarks and folders. Selecting a folder includes its descendants. Non-HTTP/HTTPS bookmarks are excluded.

Choose a folder inside the selected scope. With individual bookmarks only, existing items can sync but new root items require a selected destination folder. Move new Switchboard URLs or collections into Vivaldi Bookmarks to include them.

Not paired.

Concurrent edits pause for review in Switchboard. Changing scope requires New Pairing in Switchboard. Deletions propagate in both directions; moving a synced item outside its scope removes its counterpart. Browser recovery snapshots retain the last 20 changed batches.

Recovery snapshots contain bookmark titles and URLs. Keep them private. Disconnecting or pausing retains bookmarks in both apps.
